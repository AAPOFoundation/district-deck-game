A city of your own.

District Deck combines a Melbourne property game, an expanding world campaign and small businesses you can run from the city board.

What’s changing in V4

  • P1100 keeps its frontage when a permit changes its building.
  • Cybercab has a new low coupe silhouette and a quarterly dispatch business. Income follows service capacity, demand, energy, reliability and operating costs. These are game balance assumptions, not a forecast of real taxi returns.
  • The city includes an original synthesized score and action sounds. Open Help, then Sound for independent music and effects volume.
  • Online city rooms support an invited player, the built-in rival, or an external AI seat. The server validates plans and resolves the quarter when both players commit.

This is a development review. Independent visual, performance and full release checks are still in progress. Online world transfers and client-scored arcade rewards are not available in city rooms. Books and View plan show your completed-quarter financial totals, including Cybercab costs, purchases and profit. Detailed action outcomes are not included; older rooms may have only their latest recorded quarter. The solo campaign remains available.

Connect an AI player

Create a room with “Connect an external AI player”, then pass its invitation to your agent. The invitation grants one seat and can be used once. Keep the returned seat token private.

  1. Read the current protocol and game versions.
  2. Generate a cryptographically random 32-byte seatToken encoded as unpadded base64url (43 characters). Save it and the exact join body before sending. POST inviteToken, seatToken, protocol, stateVersion and simRevision to /api/rooms/ROOM_ID/join. Use the versions from the options response. If the reply is lost, retry the same body; the saved proof recovers the original seat. Save the returned credentials and snapshot.
  3. Authenticate subsequent calls with Authorization: Bearer SEAT_TOKEN.
  4. POST /api/rooms/ROOM_ID/get_state with the versions, current seed, quarter, and optional candidates action array. Read your seat and legal action candidates.
  5. POST /api/rooms/ROOM_ID/submit_actions with the versions, a unique requestId, the current revision, quarter, startStateHash and an actions array. This submits and commits a complete plan.
  6. GET /api/rooms/ROOM_ID/state to follow the room. After a network failure, retry the exact request with the same ID and body. After a definite conflict, refresh before making a new request.

Pacts can open a decision window after both commits. POST /decision with the current request envelope and action: {"type":"honour-pact","pactId":"…"}, or {"type":"defect","pactId":"…","replacementAction":null}. Your partner’s choice stays private until resolution.

The room protocol accepts bounded player inputs. It does not accept arbitrary state, prices, world snapshots or client-scored rewards. A rejected action does not change the game.
